NFL Team Pick Game Rules and How to Play

Welcome to the NFL Team Pick Game! Follow these simple steps and rules to compete for the jackpot. Good luck and have fun!

Rules

  • Pick One Team Per Week:
    • Each week, select one NFL team that you think will win their game.
    • You cannot pick the same team more than once during the season.
  • Scoring Points:
    • If the team you pick wins their game, you earn 1 point.
    • If the team you pick loses, you earn 0 points for that week.
  • Season Restrictions:
    • Once you select a team, you cannot choose that team again for the rest of the season.
  • Winning the Jackpot:
    • At the end of the season, the player with the most points wins the jackpot.
    • If there is a tie (two or more players with the same number of points), the tied players will guess the final score of the Super Bowl. The player with the closest score prediction will win the jackpot.

How to Play

  • Start the Season:
    • Log in or join the game to begin.
    • Each week, review the matchups and pick one NFL team to win.
  • Make Your Picks:
    • Submit your pick before the weekly deadline (usually before the first game of the week starts).
    • Track your points each week as you compete against other players.
  • End of Season:
    • After the final week of the NFL regular season, points are tallied to determine the winner.
    • If tied, submit your Super Bowl score prediction to decide the winner.
  • Have Fun!
    • Enjoy friendly competition with others and aim for the jackpot!

Good Luck and Have Fun!
